This stunning contemporary home has been sympathetically refurbished by the current owners to make the most of this light and airy accommodation which boasts high ceilings and an impressive sense of space.

As you enter through the front door you are presented with this well-proportioned entrance hall where rooms lead from and into the impressive open plan accommodation. The open plan design of this property uses all the space in zoned areas which complement modern living at its best. This large room has a modern kitchen with white gloss high level units and a sociable island with bar stools to sit at and enjoy that morning coffee. The living area has a feature fire place and there is plenty of room for a dining table too, this area provides ample space for entertaining for those large family gatherings. There is a charming conservatory with tiled floor, to enjoy a more private room, and this leads to the courtyard garden.

Make your way upstairs where you will find a generously sized landing which hosts the first set of bedrooms. To the front is a spacious double bedroom with fitted wardrobes, a further single bedroom is also at the front of the property which would make a great home office but is rather generous in size offering plenty of versatility for this space.

On the same floor, to the rear of the property is the family bathroom, with a shower over the bath. The final bedroom on this floor is another double room in size with built in wardrobes, both this room and the bathroom have views of the Cathedral, The Old Synagogue and The Marlowe Theatre.

Hidden behind double doors is another staircase which leads up to a further bedroom, offering plenty of space to truly make this another lovely double bedroom which has two good sized storage cupboards, plus additional storage in the eaves, along with an ensuite.

There is also a garage accessed from Knotts Lane via a set of electric gates.

The town centre is just a few minutes’ walk from the property and offers a wide range of shopping facilities, restaurants, entertainment, and street-side cafés, including Canterbury’s popular and recently Marlowe Theatre.

Abbots Place lies within the historic walls of the vibrant cathedral city of Canterbury, which provides an excellent range of amenities such as High Street and independent retailers, pubs, leisure facilities, two hospitals, and a comprehensive bus network.

The city is also well served by an outstanding selection of state and independent schools, three universities, and exceptional educational opportunities overall. Transport links are excellent, with two mainline railway stations offering regular services to London Victoria, Charing Cross, and Cannon Street, as well as a high-speed connection from Canterbury West to London St Pancras in under an hour.

The property is well served by local road links, with easy access to the A2/M2 to London and the coast, and convenient access to the coastal ports of Dover and Folkestone.
