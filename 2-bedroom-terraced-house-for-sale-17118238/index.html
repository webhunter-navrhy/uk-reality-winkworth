Description

This attractive and spacious two-bedroom house is in the exclusive Abbotts Lea Cottages development for the over 55s and is offered with no forward chain. Located just a short distance from Winchester City Centre and the train station this property is perfect for retirement living.

The spacious hallway has two built-in cupboards providing useful storage and provides access to the downstairs accommodation. The smart fitted kitchen lies to the front and features integrated appliances including oven, grill, gas hob as well as a dishwasher and washing machine, which are both included in the sale. To the rear, the spacious sitting room is lovely and bright courtesy of large, full-height windows and a glazed door opening out to the garden. Adjacent to the sitting room, the dining room, could alternatively be used as a bedroom in combination with the super downstairs shower room and WC, creating a perfect space for those needing accommodation all at ground-floor level.

Stairs rise to the first floor where there are two double bedrooms and a bathroom. A stairlift is currently in place and can be included in the sale or easily removed, depending on a purchaser’s preference. Both bedrooms are a good size and lie to the rear overlooking the garden, while bedroom one benefits from built-in wardrobe storage.

Outside there is a low maintenance patio garden to the rear, perfect for relaxing or dining al fresco. The property benefits from one allocated parking space.

Location:

Abbotts Lea Cottages are located on the northern fringes of Winchester, ideally located to give easy access to Winchester railway station. It is a short distance to the high street shops, boutiques, library, coffee shops, public houses, restaurants, theatre, cinema, museum and of course the city's historic cathedral. The M3 motorway, A34 and A303 are also easily accessible from this location.

Directions:

From the High Street take the second exit at the roundabout, following the one-way system down to the traffic lights. Proceed straight across and take the second right onto Worthy Lane. After approximately 0.5 miles, turn right into Abbotts Lea Cottages.
