A superb two-bedroom leasehold first-floor apartment, ideally situated on St Swithin’s Square in the heart of Lincoln. The property has been fully refurbished throughout and offers stylish, ready-to-move-into accommodation extending to approximately 769 sq ft, with the added benefit of an exceptionally generous private balcony.

The centrepiece of the flat is the bright and spacious open-plan kitchen and living room, offering plenty of space to relax, dine and entertain. The contemporary fitted kitchen features a Bosch oven, Bosch ceramic hob, a good range of storage and a practical breakfast bar, which has been thoughtfully incorporated to maximise the living space. Wood flooring runs throughout the hallway and continues seamlessly into the kitchen and living area. French doors provide plenty of natural light and open directly onto the large balcony, creating a fantastic outdoor seating and entertaining space rarely found with a city-centre flat.

The principal bedroom is a well-proportioned double and benefits from its own modern en-suite shower room. There is a further comfortable double bedroom, together with a particularly spacious family-style bathroom fitted with both a bath and separate shower enclosure.

Finished in a fresh, neutral style throughout, the flat is conveniently placed for Lincoln city centre, with its excellent range of shops, restaurants, bars and local amenities all within easy reach. Offering generous accommodation, two bathrooms and impressive outside space, this attractive property would make an ideal home for first-time buyers, professionals or investors.
